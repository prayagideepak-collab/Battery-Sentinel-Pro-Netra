# Battery Sentinel Pro Nethra

## Overview
Battery Sentinel Pro Nethra is an advanced, ultra-low power 24/7 battery and thermal monitoring Android application built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, adhering strictly to Material Design 3 and Google Play policies.

## Architecture & Core Modules
1. **Central Unit Architecture & ChargingSpeedEngine**: All device telemetry, raw incoming power (`Voltage × Current`), phone consumption, net effective power, charging speed categorization (`Slow <5W`, `Normal 5–10W`, `Fast 10–20W`, `Ultra Fast >20W`), and event transitions are strictly controlled by `ChargingSpeedEngine` within the `NetraCentralDataCenter` single source of truth.
2. **Charger Session Model (Parts 6 & 7)**: Distinct session timestamp management controlled exclusively by the Central Unit (`chargerConnectedAt`, `chargingStartedAt`, `chargingStoppedAt`, `chargerDisconnectedAt`, `dischargingStartedAt`) with strict deduplication preventing resets during telemetry fluctuations.
3. **Persistent Battery Notification (Part 7)**: One single Central Unit-driven persistent foreground notification (`BatteryMonitorService`) that automatically renders charging, discharging, or idle states, displaying raw incoming power, canonical speed categories, session durations, ETAs, and thermal readings without any local notification-side calculations.
4. **Central State & Events**: Canonical state and events (`NetraCentralState`, `NetraCentralEvent`) decouple instantaneous telemetry readings from meaningful state transitions.
5. **Live Updates & ETA**: Continuous live numeric power updates and sliding-window historical ETA estimation for both charging and discharging sessions exposed across UI, notification, and widget services without redundant polling.
6. **Centralized Announcement Engine (Part 8)**: Autonomous, event-driven announcement engine driven exclusively by `NetraCentralDataCenter` central events. Driven by a single-threaded priority queue prioritizing critical thermal events, with strict duplicate prevention, startup baseline checks (preventing startup announcement spam), and obsolete battery boundary pruning. Displays raw charging speed on-screen, but speaks the net effective charging speed category (derived from net effective power) to ensure truthfulness in audio outputs.
7. **Central Announcement Queue & Spam Control (Part 9)**: Fully integrated single-threaded Priority Queue managing announcement lifecycles (`QUEUED`, `SPEAKING`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`). Implements real-time critical thermal speech interruption, absolute event-level deduplication across multiple observers or recompositions via tracked event hashes, stale low-priority backlog compression, and automated service restart baseline guards preventing startup spam.
8. **Central Unit Controlled Media Playback (Part 10)**: Canonical integration of media state tracking (`PLAYING`, `PAUSED`, `STOPPED`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNSUPPORTED`) and action capabilities within the Central Unit (`NetraCentralDataCenter`). Manages transient audio focus requests and releases. Implements strict pause ownership: media is paused only if playing and resume commands are only sent back if Nethra itself initiated the pause. Already paused or stopped media states are completely preserved, eliminating false resumes.
9. **Central Unit Controlled Night Protection (Part 11)**: Canonical calculation of Night Protection policies executed strictly inside the Central Unit (`NetraCentralDataCenter`). Under the master switch, suppresses non-critical announcements (battery percentages, speed category, Bluetooth levels) from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM (default or custom HH:mm config), while seamlessly continuing all background telemetry, charging session calculations, and persistent notification updates. Genuinely critical events (such as critical thermal warnings) remain fully active and unsuppressed. Correctly resolves midnight-crossing configurations, rejects invalid same-hour configurations, and guarantees zero post-night backlog playback.
10. **Central Unit Controlled Bluetooth Live & Historical Devices (Part 12)**:
   - **Root Cause & Fix**: Fixed the issue where connected Bluetooth devices were not appearing by replacing unsupported `BluetoothManager.getConnectionState()` calls with genuine `BluetoothProfile` proxy binding (A2DP & Headset) and GATT connection checks, combined with ACL connection tracking broadcasts.
   - **Central Unit Ownership**: All Bluetooth state determination, validation, deduplication, and last-valid-value retention are owned exclusively by `NetraCentralDataCenter`. No UI or helper independently decides connection states.
   - **Paired ≠ Connected & Live ≠ History**: The `LIVE` view displays strictly currently connected devices. Paired but disconnected devices are kept in the `HISTORY` view and marked as "Previously Connected (Offline)" / "Not currently connected".
   - **Battery Telemetry & Visual State**: Reads genuine remote device battery levels using the standard public Android API (`getBatteryLevel()`) without manufacturing fake percentages. When battery readings become temporarily unavailable, last-valid values are retained. Device cards reflect battery levels dynamically (Very Low: Red, Low/Medium: Amber, Good: Green, High: Emerald; neutral gray for Unavailable).
   - **Persistent History**: Seamlessly persists historical peripherals using ultra-lightweight JSON preferences within the Central Unit architecture, updating records on reconnection without creating duplicate devices.
11. **Screen Consolidation & Canonical Data Surfacing (Part 13)**:
   - **Five-Tab Architecture**: Fixed five bottom navigation destinations without redundant bottom tabs:
     1. **Home**: High-level live dashboard displaying canonical battery %, charging/discharging/idle status, temperature, alerts, and quick navigation.
     2. **Battery**: Unified battery intelligence screen automatically representing Charging, Discharging, and Idle states. Features raw incoming charging power (`<5W` Slow, `5–10W` Normal, `10–20W` Fast, `>20W` Ultra Fast) without subtracting phone consumption, session timings, and an embedded "Sessions & Graph" interactive view.
     3. **Monitoring**: Central operational and diagnostic surface consolidating System/Hardware sensors (RAM, CPU, storage, thermal), App battery usage drain, and Activity/Event Logs into sub-tabs.
     4. **Devices**: Consolidated Bluetooth peripheral management with Live Connected and Historical views from Part 12.
     5. **Settings**: Central user-control surface for announcements, night protection, cutoff targets, and thresholds.
   - **Reused Status Meter**: Reused `CircularBatteryGauge` across screens to surface canonical battery levels, raw charging power, speed categories, and Central Unit-derived ETAs (charging time to full and discharging runtime remaining) without duplicate calculations.
   - **Consistent Unavailable Handling**: Eliminated blocking "telemetry unavailable" screens. Missing individual fields gracefully show "Unavailable" or "Calculating" while preserving and displaying all other valid and last-retained fields.
   - **Telemetry Consistency**: Home, Battery screen, and persistent notification consume the identical canonical Central Unit state flow.
